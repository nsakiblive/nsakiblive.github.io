CASE 03 / POWER BI

US Accident Data:
Weather & Temperature Impact

A data analytics project exploring how weather, temperature and wind conditions affected road safety using 2019 U.S. accident data merged with external weather information.

TypeData analytics
ToolPower BI
DatasetU.S. accidents + weather
Year2019 data
DASHBOARD IMAGE NEEDEDVISUAL EVIDENCE

01 / DATA WORK

Two datasets had to become one analytical story.

I cleaned and organized accident records, merged them with weather data, aligned timestamps and units, and then built the analysis around conditions such as rain, snow, fog, temperature changes and wind.

02 / DASHBOARD

Interactive enough to explore. Clear enough to explain.

  • Date-range, state and weather-type filters for exploration.
  • KPI-style summary views and comparative charts.
  • Trend views designed for non-technical interpretation.
  • Hover detail and segmented analysis around conditions and seasonal changes.
03 / FINDINGS

Weather risk was not only about extreme events.

The analysis highlighted higher accident levels during poor weather, noticeable changes around seasonal transitions and near-freezing conditions, and stronger risk where wind or gust conditions combined with rain or snow. Rural and open-road conditions appeared particularly exposed in the patterns explored.

This is an analytical portfolio project; the dashboard communicates associations and patterns rather than claiming weather alone caused individual crashes.
