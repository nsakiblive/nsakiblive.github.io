HOW I APPROACH THE WORK
01

Frame the question

Define what decision the work needs to support before collecting more information.

02

Verify the evidence

Use source checks, structured QA and explicit assumptions rather than hiding uncertainty.

03

Build the useful thing

Dataset, dashboard, report, model, deck, training manual or policy note — the format follows the problem.

04

Make the next step visible

Good analysis should reduce confusion and leave the user knowing what to do next.
